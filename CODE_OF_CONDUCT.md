# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming, regardless of experience, identity, background, or the hardware someone can access.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, specific, and helpful.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect privacy: do not request or publish credentials, private media, local paths, signing identities, or personal information.
- Assume good intent, ask for clarification, and make room for different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and persistent bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's reporting tools or contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
